Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Purpose

This application is used exclusively for personal, non-commercial backups of my own Google Drive files using rclone.

Google Drive Access

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive to download files to private local storage.

Data Storage

Downloaded files are stored locally on privately managed storage devices. Google Drive files are not published on this website or sold to third parties.

Data Sharing

No Google Drive data is intentionally shared with external third parties by this backup application.

Revoking Access

Google Drive access can be revoked at any time through Google Account security settings.
